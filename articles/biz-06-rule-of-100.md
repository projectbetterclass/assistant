**The idea.** If you're not getting customers every week, you're almost certainly not doing enough volume. His rule: 100 reach-outs a day, or $100 a day on ads, every day, for 90 days. Skill comes from volume, not the other way round.

## The loop
- A master needs less volume for the same result. But the only way to become one is volume.
- You'll be bad at first and hope to break even. Volume builds skill, skill gets more from each message, you start to like it, and you do more.
- Most people reach out once a week "when motivated", or spend $100 once and call it a test.

## Two ways to start
| | Outreach | Paid ads |
|---|---|---|
| What | you message people yourself | you pay a platform to show them |
| Rough response | about 1% reply | about 1% click |
| Costs you | time | money |

- With no budget, it's outreach. You're selling your time anyway.
- Once you have the skill, teach it. Others will be half as good, but ten people at half as good beat you alone.

**Try it this week.** Track one number: reach-outs per day. Aim for 100, spread across the three lists from the last read. Don't judge the results until you've done it daily for a few weeks; his number is 90 days. Ties to P10.
