**The argument.** In the Temple of Learning, where a slave could argue with a prince, Arkad leads a debate on how to attract good luck. Gambling isn't it. The luck worth having comes through work and trade, as opportunities, and the habit that loses them is putting off the decision.

## Where luck doesn't come from
- **The gaming tables and the races.** One young man hoped good luck meant winning at dice; another thought of the chariot races. Arkad: there, people lose more than they win, because the chances are always against the player.
- **Where it does.** In farming, honest trade and every kind of work, the chances of profit are in your favour if you keep at it.

## The missed opportunities
- **The land above the canal.** A merchant tells how, as a newly married young man, their father urged them to join a venture: twelve earners each paying a tenth of their income to buy barren land, raise water to it with three ox-driven wheels, and sell it in plots for herb gardens. The merchant hesitated, wanting new robes from the east, until it was too late. It proved more profitable than anyone had predicted.
- **The flock of sheep.** An animal buyer, locked outside the city gates one night after a wasted ten-day search for camels, was offered a flock of 900 sheep cheaply by an old farmer who needed to rush home. The buyer refused to pay until the sheep could be counted in daylight. In the morning, with the city threatened by siege, other buyers rushed out and paid nearly three times the price.
- **The lesson.** Pay a deposit as soon as you're convinced a bargain is good: it protects you from your own wavering.
- **The first step.** A herdsman began a fortune as a boy by buying a calf with one piece of silver. The step from earning by your own work to drawing income from your savings is the luckiest one; take it young.

## Procrastination is the enemy
- **The Syrian's word.** A visitor from Syria, struggling with the language, asks the word for someone who puts off what's good for them: "procrastinator". Opportunity won't wait for slow people.
- **People of action.** Good luck follows opportunity. Men and women of action are favoured by the goddess of good luck.

**The idea worth keeping.** Luck is an opportunity taken; the habit that loses it is putting off the decision.

**Try it on your work.** Name one opportunity for your AI service you've been meaning to decide on: a client to approach, an offer to test, a price to set. Decide it today, and take the first step.
