**The argument.** Warm outreach reaches people one at a time; posting content says it once and reaches everyone who knows you, and they share it. The content disappears, but the audience is the asset that compounds. Make every piece hook, retain and reward attention, give far more than you ask, and the audience grows and buys.

## Why build an audience
- **Quiet the ego, look for the lesson.** Kylie Jenner, Huda Kattan and The Rock made fortunes fast through their audiences. If someone makes more money than you, they're better at the game in some way, so learn from them.
- **Volume works.** Hormozi paid $120,000 for four calls with an influencer. The advice was simply to post more; ten times the content brought 1.2 million followers in six months.
- **Free content makes all advertising work better.** In a survey, 78% of new gym clients had watched at least two long pieces of content before booking a call.
- **The trade-offs.** Content is harder to personalize, you compete with everyone else posting, and if you stand out, people copy you.

## The content unit: hook, retain, reward
- **Hook.** A reason to look away from everything else. Pick a topic (your far past, recent past, present, what's trending, or an experience you set up), a headline with at least two of recency, relevance, celebrity, proximity, conflict, something unusual and an ongoing story, and the format that already works on that platform.
- **Retain.** Curiosity: put a question in their head. Lists ("seven ways…"), steps (actions in order, toward a goal) and stories (people want to know what happens next).
- **Reward.** Deliver on the hook's promise. Value per second matters: there's no such thing as too long, only too boring. If your audience grows, it's good; if it doesn't, it isn't yet.
- **Long content is short content linked.** Hook, retain, reward, again and again. Start short.
- **Make it for strangers.** No inside jokes: content made for people who've never heard of you is what gets shared.

## Making money from it
- **Give until they ask.** TV gives about 3.5 minutes of programme for every minute of ads; growing platforms give far more. Over-give, and people come to you: give in public, ask in private.
- **Integrated or intermittent asks.** A short ask inside the content (after a valuable moment, at the end, or in a P.S.), or pure value posts with an ask every eleventh one. Ask for your core offer or your lead magnet; if unsure, the lead magnet. One call to action at a time.
- **Depth then width, or width then depth.** Max out one platform before adding the next, or get on every platform early and grow them together. Hormozi records twice a month: about 30 short clips and two to four long videos.

## Seven lessons, and the numbers
- Say "how I", not "how to": your own experience can't be argued with.
- People need reminding more than teaching: one in five followers didn't know about a book posted daily.
- Puddles, ponds, lakes, oceans: start narrow (plumbing in one town) and widen later.
- Good content becomes a tool for your salespeople, and keeps paying customers loyal.
- People don't have short attention spans, they have higher standards. Post by hand rather than scheduling.
- **Measure growth monthly.** Followers and reach, and the monthly growth rate. Hormozi's podcast ran twice a week for four years before reaching the top 100, and became a US top 10 business podcast in year five.

**The idea worth keeping.** The audience, not the content, is what compounds: hook, retain, reward, every day, and give until they ask.

**Try it on your work.** Write one post about your AI service from something that happened to you this week: a headline with two of the seven parts, a list, steps or a story inside, and no ask. Post it by hand.
