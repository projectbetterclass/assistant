**The idea.** The biggest difference from the American videos: cold email to Dutch businesses isn't allowed without consent. Phoning a BV is. Everything else is manageable if you set it up right from the start.

## Finding clients
- **Cold email** (Telecommunicatiewet art. 11.7). Commercial email needs prior consent, also to businesses and to info@ addresses, since 1 October 2009. Existing customers may be emailed about similar services if they could opt out. [Law & More](https://lawandmore.nl/zakelijke-e-mails-of-spam-wat-bedrijven-wel-en-niet-mogen-doen/), [ACM](https://www.acm.nl/nl/telecom/zakelijk-abonnement-voor-bellen-en-internet/spam) *What you do:* don't copy US cold-email playbooks or buy email lists. Use the phone, visits, LinkedIn, referrals, trade associations and content; email after a first contact.
- **Cold calling.** Since 2021 calls need opt-in for people, including sole traders (eenmanszaak) and partnerships (VOF). A BV, NV or stichting isn't covered. [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/new-rules-for-telemarketing-in-the-netherlands) *What you do:* check the legal form in the KVK register before you call. Calling a BV is allowed.

## Using AI for clients
- **AI literacy** (AI Act art. 4). Since 2 February 2025, firms that use AI must take measures to build their staff's AI literacy. The 2026 Omnibus softened the wording but kept the duty. [KI-Campus](https://ki-campus.org/en/blog/eu-ai-act-ai-omnibus-recalibrates-article-4-ai-literacy-remains-mandatory) *What you do:* it's a real reason for firms to pay for practical AI training. Sell the results; mention the duty.
- **High-risk AI** (AI Act). AI that screens or ranks job candidates or staff is high-risk. The Omnibus moved those duties to 2 December 2027 (stand-alone systems) and 2 August 2028 (built into products). [Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/en/spain/labor-and-employment/art/digital-omnibus-on-ai-how-does-it-impact-employment-relations) *What you do:* don't make candidate scoring your first product. Writing job ads is fine.
- **Chatbot transparency** (AI Act). From August 2026 a chatbot must make clear that it is an AI. [LegalNews](https://legalnews.be/innovation-ai/het-gebruik-van-ai-chatbots-door-zorgaanbieders-hoe-datalekken-te-voorkomen-akd/) *What you do:* label every chat or voice agent you set up.
- **Privacy** (AVG/GDPR). Client data in an AI tool needs a data-processing agreement (verwerkersovereenkomst). The AP wants clear rules on what staff may paste into chatbots. [accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2025/12/ap-slaat-alarm-over-datalekken-door-ai/) *What you do:* use business plans that don't train on client data, EU hosting where you can, and a one-page AI policy for each client. Sell it as a feature.

## Your own business
- **Fake self-employment** (Wet DBA). Enforced again since 2025. In 2026 there are no default fines, but back taxes are possible. [Knab](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/handhaving-wet-dba-dit-betekent-het-voor-jou) *What you do:* sell a defined monthly deliverable to several clients, not hours to one client under their direction.
- **Starting.** Register an eenmanszaak at the KVK: about €85 one-off in 2026 (sources differ). [Finom](https://finom.co/nl-nl/blog/kosten-kvk-inschrijving/) *What you do:* do it when the first client says yes, not before.
- **VAT** (the KOR scheme). Under the KOR you charge no BTW up to €20,000 turnover a year, but you can't reclaim BTW on your costs. *What you do:* business clients reclaim BTW anyway, so for B2B the KOR mostly costs you the BTW back on your laptop and software. Ask an adviser before you choose.
- **Income tax, 2026.** Box 1: 35.75% up to €38,883, 37.56% up to €78,426, 49.5% above. A 12.7% SME profit exemption with no hours test. The €1,200 self-employed and €2,123 starter deductions need 1,225 hours a year. [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/geldzaken/belastingtarieven-2026/), [Rabobank](https://www.rabobank.nl/bedrijven/eigen-bedrijf-starten/belasting/zelfstandigenaftrek-en-startersaftrek-zo-zit-dat) *What you do:* you won't reach 1,225 hours while studying, so plan without those deductions. A BV (19% corporate tax up to €200,000) only pays off much later.
- **Student finance.** Higher-education students can earn without a cap next to student finance, and a business doesn't affect it; income-based allowances like zorgtoeslag can. [Onderneming.nl](https://www.onderneming.nl/bedrijf-starten/starten/ondernemen-als-student/) *What you do:* check DUO and your allowances once profit grows.
- **R&D tax break** (WBSO). A self-employed R&D deduction of €15,979, plus €7,996 for starters, but it needs 500 R&D hours and 1,225 hours in total. [RVO](https://rvo.nl/wbso) *What you do:* only relevant later, if you build real software full-time.
- **Training subsidy** (SLIM). SMEs can get 60% of training costs back, up to €24,999 per application. The 2026 rounds are closed. [KHN](https://khn.nl/nieuws/de-slim-subsidie-vanaf-19-augustus-kun-je-m-weer-aanvragen) *What you do:* your clients may get your training subsidised in 2027. Check the 2027 round dates.

**Try it this week.** Look up three firms you might approach in the KVK register and note each one's legal form. A BV you may phone; an eenmanszaak you reach through an introduction.
