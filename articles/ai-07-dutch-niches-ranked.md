**The idea.** The two best first niches are installation firms (desperate for capacity, almost no AI yet) and export-minded SMEs (a direct fit with your International Business degree). Accountancy firms are a strong third. Hormozi's rule for picking: number of potential customers × gross profit per customer × ease of delivery.

## The two top picks
**Installers and technical services** (heating, heat pumps, solar, electrical). *Top pick if you can reach 3–5 owners.*
- **The pain:** about 29,400 open vacancies; over 60% of firms hiring; capacity, not demand, limits growth.
- **AI use now:** construction 4.6–7%.
- **Competition:** generic field-service software; few AI services built for them.
- **What you'd deliver each month:** lead response within an hour, quote follow-ups, review requests and replies, job ads.

**Export-minded SMEs** (webshops and manufacturers selling to Germany, Belgium and France). *Top pick for your degree; check the pain in interviews.*
- **The pain:** Germany takes just over a fifth of Dutch goods exports (€655bn in 2025). [CBS](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/42/export-groeit-met-bijna-5-procent-in-augustus)
- **AI use now:** varies.
- **Competition:** translation agencies, DeepL, the marketplaces' own tools.
- **What you'd deliver each month:** product listings, quotes and customer replies in German and French, checked by a person.

## A strong third
**Accountancy and bookkeeping firms.** *Also the classic roll-up target.*
- **The pain:** 31,845 firms (Q2 2026); private equity is buying the big ones; staff are scarce. [Accountancy Vanmorgen](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2026/06/10/partner-fiscount-drang-om-eigen-kantoor-te-starten-onverminderd-sterk/)
- **AI use now:** rising, through their software.
- **Competition:** Exact, Twinfield and the other packages are adding AI themselves.
- **What you'd deliver each month:** chasing missing documents, client emails, month-end prep for the messiest clients.

## Medium
- **Estate agents (makelaars).** Funda is raising listing prices (the cheapest package to €231 from €110 for NVM members) and selling its own AI styler. [NL Times](https://nltimes.nl/2026/06/19/housing-site-funda-launching-new-website-ai-tool-attract-buyers-cooling-market) Funda and Realworks build AI in. You'd deliver listing texts in Dutch and English, social posts and buyer follow-up. *Squeezed by the platforms.*
- **Recruitment and staffing agencies.** Hiring is still hard: 95 vacancies per 100 unemployed. Many HR-tech tools already. You'd deliver job ads, candidate outreach and interview summaries. *Never rank candidates: that's high-risk under the AI Act.*

## Weak, or skip
- **Hospitality (horeca).** Staff shortages and thin margins; AI use 4.2–6%. AI phone receptionists already cost €23–300 a month. [Beancount](https://beancount.io/nl/blog/2026/07/16/ai-phone-agents-receptionist-small-business-guide) *Weak: low budgets, commoditised tools.*
- **GP and physio practices.** 36% of GP practices already use AI voice reporting, up from 14% a year before. [Bright, citing Nivel](https://www.bright.nl/nieuws/2122941/honderden-nederlandse-huisartsen-gebruiken-ai.html) Specialist Dutch vendors link into patient records. *Skip: validated, but regulated and taken.*

## What to check in your interviews
- Who has the pain *and* the budget: firms with 5–50 staff, not one-person businesses.
- One task that recurs every week in every firm, done the same way. Hormozi's "inch wide and a mile deep".
- What it costs them now, in hours or lost jobs. At €47.90 an hour, 15 hours a month is worth about €700.
- Whether you can get introductions: family, a part-time job, your thesis company, a trade association.

**Try it this week.** For each of the top three niches, write down who you already know there, even at one remove. The niche with the most names is your first pick, whatever the ranking says.
