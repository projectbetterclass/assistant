**The idea.** You already know enough people to start. Message them one by one, like a human, a hundred a day. Don't sell to them; ask who they know. Take your first five on for free in return for feedback and a review. Then raise the price in steps until you're full.

## The ten steps
1. **Make three lists:** every email contact, every social account you can message, every phone contact. It's more than you think.
2. **Pick one platform:** whichever has the most of them.
3. **Make it personal.** Thirty seconds on their profile: a new baby, a new job, a move. Just show you're not blasting everyone.
4. **Reach out to 100 a day.** The first message is the hard one; the second takes two minutes.
5. **When they reply, use ACA:** acknowledge ("you have two kids"), compliment ("you must be a super mum, working full time with two kids"), ask a question that leads to what you do ("how do you find time to eat well and work out?").
6. **Ask for their friends, not their money.** "Do you know anyone looking for X? I'm opening a few spots." It's a favour, not a pitch.
7. **Make it free, honestly.** Five free case studies, if they (1) actually use it, (2) give feedback, (3) leave a review if it's earned. If someone won't take it even free, ask why. Those are hidden costs you can remove, and later charge more for.
8. **Go back to the top of the list.** Referrals bring free leads. "Reach out to 3,000 people with a free thing and you'll get five."
9. **Start charging once people refer you.** The next five pay 80% less than full price, then 60, 40, 20, full price, and above it once you're worth more.
10. **Keep them warm.** Check in, share results and reviews, until people start asking you.

## Why free first
- You need reps, and you aren't good yet. You get good by doing more.
- It isn't really free: they pay with time, effort and the review. (When Buffett offered to work for Ben Graham for free, Graham said he was overpriced.)
- When the spots fill, the scarcity is real. You can tell a free client someone is now paying full price for their spot.

**Watch for.** Half the video promotes the launch of his book $100M Leads; these steps are its warm-outreach method. Free works for a service you can deliver to five people. Don't run it for months.

**Try it this week.** Make the three lists and count them. Write your one-line offer in his template: "I help [who] get [dream outcome] in [time] without [effort or sacrifice]." Send the first 20 personal messages. Ties to P06 (commitment) and P10 (do things that don't scale).
