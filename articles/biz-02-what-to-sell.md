**The idea.** There are only seven kinds of things anyone sells, each in a physical and a digital form. With little money, you'll almost always start by selling your time as a service. Pick the problem you can solve best with what you already know, then shape how you deliver it.

## The seven things you can sell
| What | Physical | Digital |
|---|---|---|
| Products | water bottles | a PDF |
| Services | mowing a lawn, fixing a car | making ads, buying media |
| Access | leasing a building | a content library (Netflix) |
| Attention | a billboard | eyeballs on Facebook or Google |
| Risk | insuring a building | cyber insurance, a paid guarantee (AppleCare) |
| Money | cash, loans | digital money |
| Endorsement | your logo on a shirt, for a cut | a paid check mark |

A business can mix them. A concert sells access, drinks, shirts and the recording.

## Picking yours
- **You know more than you think.** What your parents did, odd jobs, the people you grew up around. His mom is an eye surgeon, so he knows a lot about eyes.
- **With about $100, sell your time.** Physical products and software usually need money up front. A service doesn't.
- **The sweet spot:** the person you can give the most value to, for the least cost to you.

## Then shape it: the delivery cube
1. **Group size:** one-to-one, small group, or one-to-many.
2. **Who does the work:** they do it with your guide, you do it with them, or you do it for them. Done-for-you is easiest to sell and hardest to deliver.
3. **Support:** chat, email, phone, video call. More scalable usually means less valuable.
4. **Format:** live or recorded, in person or online, video, audio or text. Live and in person feels most valuable and scales least.
5. **Speed:** hours open, days a week, how fast you answer.
6. **The 10x and one-tenth test:** what would make it worth ten times the price? What could you strip out to deliver the same value at a tenth of it?

**Try it this week.** List three things you already know from home, jobs or study. For each, name one person with a painful problem you could help one-to-one. Run the 10x test on the best one. Ties to P02 (a starving crowd) and P07 (the value equation).
