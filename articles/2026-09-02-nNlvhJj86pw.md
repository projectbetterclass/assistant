**The claim.** Japan's 30-year bond yield jumped to 4.18%, the highest ever. The "widow maker" trade, betting against Japanese bonds, finally paid off. Felix sees Japan as about ten years ahead of the US on the same road: too much debt, a central bank buying it with new money, and the currency paying the price. And it's happening during the biggest bubble since 2000.

## His chain of events
- **Japan:** debt about 200% of the economy. The Bank of Japan kept rates down by buying bonds, and the yen bled. Now yields are breaking out, and Japanese stocks lost $200B+ in a day, led by tech.
- **Everywhere:** global yields are the highest since 2000. The US 30-year touched 5.3% (highest since 2007), the UK's is the highest since 1998, and Australia's a 15-year high. The FT called it a global bond sell-off.
- **The US bill:** $40T of debt, with interest now costing more than the military. Borrowing to pay interest is "a Ponzi scheme". The exits are growth, default or inflation, and he expects inflation.
- **Money printing:** world money supply is $150T, +50% since 2020. A 1971 dollar buys 12 cents of today's goods.
- **The AI bubble:** AI companies borrowed $410B this year, betting on falling rates, and rates are rising. In 2000 the "can't lose" internet kings were Cisco, Yahoo and AOL. The NASDAQ fell 78% and took 15 years to recover, so $100K at 55 becomes $22K.
- **His guess at the "script":** tough talk from Fed chair Warsh (hike odds 36% → 70% in three days), then an Iran deal, cheaper oil, rate cuts and a rally. He says it's a guess.

## Why "gold isn't ready"
- Far more paper gold (futures, swaps, fund units) trades than there is metal to deliver. If everyone asks for metal at once, there isn't enough.
- Many "gold" funds only track the price, so check what yours actually holds.
