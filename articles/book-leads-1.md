**The argument.** To make money you need three pieces: stuff to sell (the offer), people to sell it to (leads), and getting them to buy (sales). $100M Offers covered the first. This book covers the second, because strangers can only buy from you if they know you exist, and the way to make that happen is advertising.

## Why leads come first
- **Advertising.** The process of making known: letting strangers know about the stuff you sell. More people knowing means more sales.
- **Lots of leads forgive a lot.** A weak product, weak selling, plenty of mistakes: with enough leads you get endless chances to get it right.
- **Two ways to grow.** Any business grows by getting more customers or by making each one worth more. This book is about the first.
- **More, better, cheaper, reliably.** You get more customers with more leads, better leads and cheaper leads, coming reliably from lots of places. All else equal, double the leads and you double the business.

## The story: $150,000 in refunds
- In 2017, a few months after a partner walked off with all the money from the sale of Hormozi's gyms, the gym-launch business was hit with $150,000 in refunds: two gym owners had told the new members to cancel.
- An online fitness offer brought in $1,000 a day, not enough. Then a broke gym owner begged to be shown how to get leads, and paid $6,000 for the advertising system.
- Hormozi called the other gyms on the list. All eight said yes, $60,000 in one day. The next 30 days made $215,000 in profit, and that year $6.8 million in revenue.
- **Giving away the secrets.** The thing that scared Hormozi most, handing over the system, was the breakthrough. And the lesson drawn from it: knowing how to get leads gave second, third and fifth chances. "I cannot lose if I do not quit."

- **Section II.** How advertising really works, and which kind of lead to go after.
- **The Core Four.** Section III: the only four ways to get leads.
- **Lead getters.** Section IV: customers, employees, agencies and affiliates who get leads for you.

**The idea worth keeping.** Most businesses don't have a product problem: not enough people know they exist. Get more leads, and most other problems get easier to fix.

**Try it on your work.** Write down how many people heard about your AI service last week, by name if you can. Then write the one thing you could do next week to double it.
