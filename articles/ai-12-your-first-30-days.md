**The idea.** The first month is for interviews and one free pilot, not for building. Each line below is a piece on this branch: start it from there, time it, and tick it off.

## Week 1
- Pick two niches from the ranked list (part 7) where you can get introductions.
- List 30 owners or managers you can reach: family, a job, your school's network, second-degree LinkedIn contacts.
- Check each firm's legal form in the KVK register before phoning: a BV may be called, a sole trader needs consent.
- Ask your thesis supervisor whether your graduation project can be on AI adoption in one of the two niches.
- Reread The Mom Test notes on your Bookshelf before the first interview.

## Weeks 2–3
- Do 10 interviews per niche. Ask about last week's admin, what it cost in hours or lost jobs, and what they've tried. Don't pitch.
- Write down every recurring task with its hours and who does it.

## Week 4
- Choose the one task that at least 6 of 10 owners named, with hours attached.
- Run it by hand for one firm with off-the-shelf tools (a business AI plan plus Zapier, Make or n8n), and log every edge case.
- Offer three firms the free 60-day pilot in exchange for feedback, a testimonial and a referral.
- Register your eenmanszaak at the KVK when the first firm says yes.
- Log hours and results per client from day one: they become your proof and your price.

## Questions for the interviews
From The Mom Test: ask about their life, not your idea; about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future; and talk less, listen more. Lesson P04 practises the same skill.
- "Walk me through last week's admin. What took longest?"
- "What did that cost you, in hours or in jobs you missed?"
- "What have you already tried? What did it cost?"
- "Who else should I talk to?"
- "Is there anything else I should have asked?"

**Try it this week.** Do Week 1. The first piece, picking two niches, takes one 14-minute slot.
