**The argument.** Every job comes down to a handful of results you're fully responsible for, and a few core tasks that make up most of your value. One of the biggest reasons people put off a task is feeling they aren't good at it, so the cure for procrastination is often getting better at the thing that matters.

## Key result areas
- **What they are.** The results you're completely responsible for: if you don't do them, nobody will. Most jobs have five to seven. For a salesperson: finding prospects, building rapport, finding needs, presenting, answering objections, closing, and getting repeat sales and referrals.
- **Grade yourself.** Score yourself from 1 to 10 in each one.
- **The weakest link.** Your weakest key result area sets the height at which you can use all your other skills.
- **The big question.** What one skill, if you did it excellently and consistently, would have the biggest positive effect on your work?

## The law of three
- **Three tasks.** Three core tasks make up most of your contribution: Tracy puts it at about 90%.
- **Find them.** List everything you do in a week or a month. Ask: if I could do only one thing on this list all day, which would add the most value? Then the second, then the third.
- **Spend your time there.** Do more of those three, and delegate or drop the rest.
- **The quick list.** Write your three most important goals in 30 seconds. Whatever comes out first is usually what matters.

## Upgrade your skills, use your talents
- **Keep learning.** Read in your field every day (Tracy suggests an hour), take courses, and listen to audio in the car or while you travel. Getting better at a key task makes it easier to start.
- **Special talents.** What are you very good at? What comes easily to you and is hard for others? What has been most responsible for your success so far? Put your best energy there.

**The idea worth keeping.** Your results come from a few key areas and three core tasks; the weakest area holds back all the others, so get better at it.

**Try it on your work.** List the five to seven key result areas of your AI service and grade each from 1 to 10. Name the weakest one, and one thing you'll do this month to raise its score.
