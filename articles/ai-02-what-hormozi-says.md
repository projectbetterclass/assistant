**The idea.** Hormozi backs AI-run services for small businesses and warns first-time founders off software. His test for any use of AI: "Though I am using AI, am I making more money?" These are the eleven points from 558 Alex Hormozi and 299 Leila Hormozi transcripts that bear on your question.

## Eleven points
1. **Small-business AI is the opening; custom work isn't.** "AI implementation for Main Street is an enormous opportunity", but "being the custom guy is tough": "all things to all people is nothing to no one." Pick one industry and one problem that is the same in every firm. [81 Minutes of Money Making Advice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_s0ekwPK5g)
2. **Choose the problem by numbers.** "Value created times ease of implementation", where value is the number of potential customers × gross profit per customer. Get the insight from your own industry experience or an insider partner. [81 Minutes of Money Making Advice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_s0ekwPK5g)
3. **Charge the old price.** People still pay the old labour price (the example: $2,000 a month) while AI delivers it for $50. That gap is the margin, and "one person now can bring millions in revenue". [How to Win With AI in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9q5ojtkqsBs)
4. **Sell the outcome, hide the tool.** "The fact that I'm using AI is irrelevant. You just sell the outcome." Clients want it faster, cheaper, better and less risky. "Don't say we're an AI design firm. Just be a design firm." [How to Use AI in Your Business in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fr78adfAnuA), [4 Ways To Get Ahead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s7QA1TJKlbQ)
5. **Most AI use earns nothing.** AI doesn't cancel the other kinds of leverage: capital, media, people, better decisions. "The only thing more efficient than automation is elimination." Token bills rise whether or not revenue does. [Why AI won't make you rich in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WttZ6b-KUP4), [Social Media Strategy 2027](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZTSI3DDP_4A)
6. **AI-first means data-first.** "You have to be a data first company. Because AI works with data." The fix for a people-heavy firm: redesign the workflow, cut headcount about 50%, double the margin. [Watch This If You Have a Service Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqo0lVveh98)
7. **Software is a hard game for non-software people.** More than 10% churn a year means "you're just a service business". Competitors copy you fast. Product-market fit means people "buy, use, tell". "You're either service or you're tech. Period." [Starting a software company is a TERRIBLE idea](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pw1PcHfYP2w)
8. **Know the niche before you sell to it.** Leila met a founder building "AI for gyms" with no gym background and no AI background. Investors look for founders who lived the problem. Expect about 5 years before it really works. [4 Simple Businesses (Leila)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b6j4EGvcXBw), [10 Minute Millionaire](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VgEvIIpDYG8)
9. **Proof and brand are the moat now.** An AI avatar "can never have done something in the real world." Business buyers want proof, so document client results. "In the world of AI, your brand is your moat." [14 Years of Marketing Advice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDR-R--4HhM), [26 Harsh Lessons](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YONIUpk-kAs)
10. **Start expensive, with few clients.** "Sell extremely expensive stuff to a select few or super cheap to everyone. The middle is where people die." Pick buyers who have money, feel the pain and are easy to reach. [If I Started A Business in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uWdIgftpvBI)
11. **Hormozi's own AI software was paid per result.** Allen used AI to work leads and was paid per appointment that showed up. Leila says it cost about $2M in development. [I Built 4 Businesses In A Row](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mqqbuM9sAk), [From Minimum Wage to $100M (Leila)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmaL_7j0uWk)

## The "perfect business" test
Hormozi scores any idea on five things: sticky (customers keep paying), high margin, a growing market, simple to run, and hard to copy. Part 6 uses it to score your options. From Skool's data, the same video adds that over 20% of members leave in month 1, about 10% around month 3, and churn falls to about 2% a month after month 6. [What Makes The Perfect Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3fsJFUvA6Ts)
