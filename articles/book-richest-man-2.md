**The argument.** The king of Babylon sees that a few are rich and most are poor, and asks Arkad to teach the city. Arkad teaches a class of a hundred, one cure a day for seven days. Together they turn a lean purse into a fat one: keep a tenth, live on a budget, make the savings earn and keep them safe, own your home, provide for the future, and grow what you can earn.

## Cures one and two: keep a tenth, live on the rest
- **Start thy purse to fattening.** For every ten coins you put in your purse, take out only nine. Arkad's students find they don't miss the tenth.
- **Control thy expenditures.** What we call "necessary expenses" always grow to equal our income, unless we fight it. Make a budget: list what you want to spend on, keep the things you really need within nine-tenths of your income, and cut the rest. A budget is there to protect what you value most, not to stop all pleasure.

## Cures three and four: make it earn, keep it safe
- **Make thy gold multiply.** Put your savings to work, earning more. Your wealth isn't the coins in your purse, but the income they bring in.
- **Guard thy treasures from loss.** Safety of the principal comes first. Don't be tempted by big promised returns, and before investing, take advice from people experienced in handling money.

## Cures five to seven: home, future, earning power
- **Make of thy dwelling a profitable investment.** Own your home: what would have gone to a landlord builds something of your own.
- **Insure a future income.** Provide for when you're old, and for your family if you die: savings, investments and insurance that keep paying.
- **Increase thy ability to earn.** Want specific things, get more skilled at your work, pay your debts promptly, look after your family, and respect yourself. The more you know, the more you can earn.

**The idea worth keeping.** Keep a tenth, live on the rest by a budget, make the tenth earn and keep it safe, own your home, provide for the future, and keep growing what you can earn.

**Try it on your work.** Score your own money from 1 to 10 against each of the seven cures, using your spending view and FIRE plan. Pick the lowest score, and write one step you'll take on it this month.
