**The claim.** Five headlines are one story: the money system is being quietly reset. Trump is leaning on the Fed to cut by Sep 16, countries are bringing their gold home, steady buyers are stepping back from US debt, and the big banks are building a new digital dollar. Felix says not to panic, but to stop treating cash and the S&P 500 as "safe".

## The five pieces
- **The Fed:** Trump told the Fed to cut rates or he'll start a trade war with deficit countries, and to "be patriots". The market expects a hike on Sep 16.
- **Gold leaving:** the Netherlands moved 86 tons out of North America, France finished pulling 129 tons out of New York, Germany ~300 tons. In 1971 the Dutch asked for their gold, Paul Volcker told them they were "rocking the boat", and a month later Nixon closed the gold window. A decade of inflation followed.
- **Debt buyers stepping back:** Norway's $2.3T oil fund proposed cutting ~$80B of US Treasuries. Japan and the Gulf states are pulling back too. Fewer lenders means higher rates on everything.
- **The digital dollar:** on Sep 1, 21 big banks (Goldman, Citi, Bank of America, UBS and others) formed a company to launch a dollar stablecoin in 2027 under the GENIUS Act. JPMorgan is building its own. Whoever builds the rails decides how money moves.
- **Japan:** the yen scare that wiped about $1T off US stocks in 40 minutes was "the first tremor".
