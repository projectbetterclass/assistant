**The idea.** The gap is in ordinary small firms. Tech companies already use AI; builders, installers and restaurants mostly don't, while their staff are scarce and among the most expensive in Europe.

## Who uses AI already
| Sector | Firms with 2+ staff, 2025 | Micro firms with 2–9 staff, 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information and communication | 54% | 50.4% |
| Construction | 7% | 4.6% |
| Hospitality (horeca) | 6% | 4.2% |
| All sectors | about 1 in 6 | — |

The CBS figures are provisional. In 2024, 59% of firms with 500+ staff used AI, against about 18% of firms with 10–19. [CBS](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2025/50/bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop), [CBS micro firms](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/longread/rapportages/2026/gebruik-van-ai-technologie-door-nederlandse-microbedrijven?onepage=true), [CBS 2024](https://longreads.cbs.nl/the-netherlands-in-numbers-2025/how-many-companies-in-the-netherlands-are-using-ai)

## Ten numbers, and why each matters to you
- **€47.90 for an hour of staff time** (EU average €34.90), the third highest in the EU in 2025. [Eurostat figures via BCCI](https://www.infobusiness.bcci.bg/bulgaria-has-lowest-labour-costs-in-eu-in-2025-but-highest-annual-increase.html) *For you:* a service that saves 15 hours a month is worth about €700 to a firm. That's your pricing anchor.
- **95 vacancies per 100 unemployed** in Q2 2026; the peak was 142 in 2022. [CBS](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/31/werkloosheid-gedaald-in-tweede-kwartaal-2026) *For you:* "you can't hire, so automate" still lands.
- **About 29,400 open vacancies at installers** in mid-2025, with over 60% of firms hiring. [Wij Techniek](https://trendfiles.wij-techniek.nl/wp-content/uploads/Uitkomsten-bedrijvenenquete-Wij-Techniek-2025.pdf) *For you:* Techniek Nederland says capacity, not demand, now limits the sector, and it is turning to AI. [Outlook](https://trendfiles.wij-techniek.nl/wp-content/uploads/economische-vooruitzichten-2027-en-verder-techniek-nederland.pdf)
- **1,654,300 SMEs** in Q1 2026, about 81% of them one-person firms. [Staat van het mkb](https://www.eur.nl/en/media/2026-09-ecexezkdestaatvhnederlandsemkb2026-1509) *For you:* about 300,000 firms have staff. Those are your buyers.
- **Half of entrepreneurs don't know the AI Act,** 7% know it well, and 3 in 10 use ChatGPT or Copilot (KVK, 651 surveyed, June 2025). [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/pers/kvk-onderzoek-ondernemers-zetten-nauwelijks-stappen-rond-europese-ai-wetgeving/) *For you:* self-employed people said they don't know where to start.
- **Over 500,000 small firms are selling or plan to:** 12% are selling, 23% plan to within 5 years. [Kleinbedrijf Index, Feb 2026](https://www.onl.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/KB-index_Editie-19_Februari-2026.pdf) *For you:* serve an industry now; buying a firm later becomes an option (part 6).
- **Data leaks from AI are rising.** The Dutch privacy regulator (AP) reports fast-rising breach reports from staff pasting data into chatbots, more in 2025 than in 2024. [accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2025/12/ap-slaat-alarm-over-datalekken-door-ai/) *For you:* a privacy-safe setup is part of what you sell.
- **A 56% wage premium** for workers with AI skills in the same job, worldwide, 2025. [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/ai/ai-jobs-barometer.html) *For you:* the same skill pays off on your Career branch.
- **1,296 Dutch AI startups** and Europe's highest AI talent density, but only 21.2% grow into scale-ups, against 31.1% EU-wide. [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/lists/ai-startups-netherlands/), [TNO](https://www.tno.nl/en/newsroom/2026/02/state-dutch-tech-2026/) *For you:* building AI tech is crowded; applying it in ordinary firms isn't.
- **A Dutch-language model is coming.** GPT-NL is in pilots with its first organisations; a public version is planned for late 2026. [Dutch IT Channel](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/news/725960/nederlands-taalmodel-gpt-nl-bij-eerste-gebruikers-getest) *For you:* Dutch-language, EU-hosted AI is becoming a selling point.

**Try it this week.** Ask one person you know who works at a small firm (5–50 staff) a single question: what job there is still done by hand every week, and how long it takes.
