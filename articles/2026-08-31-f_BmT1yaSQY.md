**The claim.** NVIDIA's latest quarter showed AI spending spreading beyond the tech giants. Sales to AI clouds, industry and enterprise grew 138%, faster than to the hyperscalers (102%), for the first time. The "neoclouds" that rent out NVIDIA chips are set to almost triple capacity this year, from ~3 GW to 8 GW. He compares the three listed ones and would pick IREN if he could own only one.

## NVIDIA's quarter, briefly
- Revenue $96.2B (+106%), with growth accelerating four quarters in a row: 62%, 73%, 85%, 106%. Operating margin 66%, against 34% for Alphabet and ~17% for AMD.
- About 15% of its earnings growth came from $7.8B of gains on stakes in other AI companies.
- Supply commitments jumped to $279B, mostly memory. He reads that as memory makers selling everything they can make.

## The three neoclouds
| | CoreWeave (CRWV) | Nebius (NBIS) | IREN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quarterly revenue | $2.6B (+112%) | $0.58B (+454%) | $137M (AI $70.5M) |
| Backlog | $104B | $37.5B | $16.6B |
| Active / contracted power | 1.5 / 4.2 GW | ~0.8–1 / 5 GW | ~0.3 / 5+ GW |
| Net debt | ~$46B | ~$2B | ~$1.9B |
| Interest as % of revenue | ~25% | ~20% | ~18% |
| Value / year-end run rate | 4.9× | 7.4× | 4.0× |
| Big customers | OpenAI, Microsoft, Meta ($21B) | Microsoft ($17.4B), Meta (up to $27B) | Microsoft ($9.7B), NVIDIA ($3.4B) |

- **CoreWeave:** the biggest and first to get new NVIDIA chips. NVIDIA will buy any unsold capacity through 2032, and it invested $2B at $87 a share, above today's ~$84. But interest turned a $49M operating loss into a $626M net loss. *His pick for newer investors or anyone near retirement.*
- **Nebius:** customers prepay 50–60% of equipment, $9B+ this year. It's the most expensive of the three and is also raising money by selling shares (dilution).
- **IREN:** a former Bitcoin miner that already owns land and power. Its AI revenue doubled in a quarter. A $684M loss came mostly from writing off Bitcoin gear. *His pick for the most upside*, and he thinks the execution risk is priced in.

**Risks he names.** CoreWeave's debt, Nebius' price and dilution, and whether IREN can turn contracted power into revenue. The numbers come from his own table across different fiscal calendars. A sponsor slot for a dictation app runs in the middle.

**How the call aged.** Since Aug 31: IREN −5.2%, CoreWeave −3.3%, Nebius +7.1%, NVIDIA +3.8%, S&P 500 +1.6%. His top pick is behind so far.

**Aswath check.** He compares enterprise value to revenue, which is the right move for a company in debt. But price-to-run-rate is still pricing (L22). The real test is the return on all that capital: how much does a megawatt earn after interest and replacing chips every few years (L10, L19)? For CoreWeave, interest at 25% of revenue makes the cost of debt (L06) the whole story.
