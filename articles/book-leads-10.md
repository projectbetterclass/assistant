**The argument.** An affiliate is an independent business that tells its own audience to buy your stuff. You get affiliates the way you get customers, with an offer, but the offer is a fast, simple way to make money promoting you. Recruit them, get them launching, then build you into what they sell, and each one becomes a stream of leads that doesn't stop.

## The story: $3 million of supplements in a warehouse
- In 2018 Prestige Labs launched to Hormozi's gym-owner customers as affiliates: over a year of work, $1 million of affiliate software and training, and $3 million of stock.
- Three weeks in: $150,000 in sales. At that rate it would take five years to break even. Week four: over $450,000, and $380,000 to $450,000 every week after. No ads and no sales team: the affiliates did it all.

## Why an affiliate army
- **The maths of leverage.** Ten customers a month at $10,000 is $1.2 million a year, flat. Ten affiliates a month who each bring one $10,000 customer a month is $7.8 million in the same year, still growing.
- **Super affiliates.** Hormozi's software company Allen had three levels: super affiliates brought agencies, agencies brought local businesses, local businesses brought leads. One super affiliate added $12,500 a month, then $25,000, then $37,500. A few of them took it to $1.7 million a month within six months.

## Six steps to an affiliate army
1. **Find your ideal affiliates.** Businesses whose warm audience is full of your kind of customer. Ask what your customers buy, from whom, where they go and what they like to do. Sort them into software, products, equipment, services, groups and events.
2. **Make them an offer.** Callout, value, call to action, with the affiliate as the customer: more money and more leads from customers they already have, likely to work because those customers already want it, nothing to build or support, and they can start tomorrow.
3. **Qualify them.** If they pay, they pay attention. Make them a customer (buy and use it, in bulk for physical products) and an expert (pay for training and certification: 10 to 20% of what an average affiliate makes in a year).
4. **Decide what to pay.** Pay for exactly what you want: new and repeat customers. Work out the most you can pay per customer at 3 to 1, then tier it: 25% when they sign up, 50% once active, 100% for steady top performers. The blended payout falls below the maximum. Pay in product where you can: "sell three, get it free".
5. **Get them advertising.** Do all the work for them, then launch: **whisper, tease, shout.** Whisper: curiosity and behind the scenes, months ahead. Tease: reveal the product, the date and the value, from 30 days out. Shout: calls to action with bonuses and urgency, from three days out. Like a film's trailers.
6. **Keep them advertising: integrate.** From easiest to hardest: they give away your lead magnet with every sale of theirs (a free massage with each training package, white-labelled meal plans), they sell your lead magnet and keep the money, or they sell your core offer and you split it, ideally forever.

## Case studies, and the numbers
- **A $50 million business-setup company** gives every affiliate's customer a free LLC set-up, then sells bookkeeping and tax services. No paid ads.
- **Prestige Labs** gets gym owners to run a free 28-day challenge, then sell supplements at every new member's nutrition orientation.
- **Chiropractors** run workshops at gyms (the gym keeps the ticket money), then get one or two adjustments included with every new membership.
- **Employers are affiliates too.** A free massage in every new hire's welcome pack.
- **Returns.** Compare the cost to get an affiliate with the gross profit of all the customers they bring. $4,000 to recruit one who sells $120,000 a year at 75% margin, paid 40% of gross profit, leaves $54,000: about 13.5 to 1.
- **Start with 10 to 20.** Get them results, fix the offer and the launch, then turn their results into the ads for your next affiliates.

## The four lead getters, together
- Referrals have the biggest potential for cheap, exponential growth; employees run things on your behalf; agencies teach skills you keep; affiliates, once going, run on their own.
- Expect a new lead source to take three to six months to crack. Pick a strategy and stick with it rather than switching after a few losses.

**The idea worth keeping.** Find the businesses that already have your customers, give them a simple way to make money with you, and treat them like customers.

**Try it on your work.** List five businesses that already serve the small firms your AI service is for. For one of them, write the affiliate offer: what they get, what you'd pay per customer, and which lead magnet of yours they could give away.
