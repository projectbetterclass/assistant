**The claim.** In January 2025, the free DeepSeek model wiped $589B off NVIDIA in a day on the fear that cheap models kill chip demand. The opposite happened. Free models now catch the best paid ones within months *and* do real work, which makes AI cheap enough for millions of new jobs. That means more chips, not fewer. Carbutt owns a "reasonable amount" of AMD and a little NVIDIA.

## Open models are closing the gap faster
| Era | Free model | Time to catch the best paid one |
|---|---|---|
| Scaling | Llama 2 70B | ~14 months |
| Reasoning | DeepSeek R1 | 8.5 months |
| Agents | Kimi K2.6 | 4.5 months |

- These are SemiAnalysis' own tests, not the labs' self-reported scores. Open models have never taken the lead, though. Big jumps still come from closed labs.
- The change that matters: GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 now do real coding and agent work, the work that took Anthropic past $65B a year.

## Jevons paradox
- In 1865, more efficient steam engines made Britain burn *more* coal, because coal power became worth using in many more places.
- Same with AI. At a tenth of the price, it's worth using for cheap customer service, small-business paperwork, agents running all day, and models inside devices.
- Proof: Fireworks, one provider of mostly open models, runs 40T+ tokens a day, about double OpenAI's whole API.
- AMD raised its server-CPU market forecast from ~$60B to $120B+, then $200B by 2030, as agents push the CPU-to-GPU ratio from 1:8 toward 1:1. NVIDIA sees a new $200B CPU market. NVIDIA grew 106% with China at almost zero.
- AMD targets non-GAAP earnings per share of $20+ in 3–5 years, from $4.17 in 2025. He quotes $2.68 (a different measure) to get "sevenfold". On the same measure it's about fivefold.

**Risks he names.** Jevons isn't a law, and there may be a ceiling on how much AI the world wants. More demand at lower prices can mean less profit, since NVIDIA's margins depend on customers having few alternatives. The big forecasts come from the sellers. NVIDIA's CUDA software lead may keep AMD out. And if adoption flattens, the industry is stuck with years of pre-ordered equipment. A sponsor slot for a budgeting app sits in the middle.

**How the call aged.** Since Sep 1: AMD +32.3%, NVIDIA +5.4%, S&P 500 +2.4%. A good month for his bigger holding.

**Aswath check.** Jevons is about volume. A valuation also needs price and margin (L08). If AMD earns $20 a share in five years, what is that worth today after the risk it doesn't (L14)? Its rise since Sep 1 already prices in part of that path.
