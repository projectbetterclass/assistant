**The claim.** The bond market (~$160T) is bigger than the stock market and quietly sets the price of everything else. The biggest borrower on Earth is now stuck: the US will most likely inflate its debt away, and savers pay for that. Learn two numbers, the riskfree rate and the spread, and you'll see trouble coming before stock investors do.

## Bonds in plain English
- A bond is a loan. You're the lender, and you get interest plus your money back at the end.
- **Price and yield move opposite ways.** If new bonds pay 5%, your old 2% bond has to sell for less. When fear sends everyone into safe bonds, prices rise and yields fall.
- **The riskfree rate:** what the US government pays is the floor every investment must beat. When it rises, everything risky gets repriced lower, even on a day with no news about your companies.
- **The spread:** what shaky borrowers pay on top. When it widens, say from 7% to 9% against a 5% Treasury, lenders smell trouble. It has warned of most crises weeks or months ahead.

## Why the US is "trapped"
- Debt passed $40T, $1T added in five months. Interest now costs more than the military, and only Social Security is bigger.
- Borrowing to pay interest makes more debt and more interest. Much of it is short-term, so it rolls over at today's rates.
- The exits are growth (too big to outgrow), default (unthinkable) or inflation, "the quiet door". A 1971 dollar buys 12 cents of goods today.
- **Japan is ten years ahead:** debt ~200% of its economy (US ~120%). Betting against its bonds was the "widow maker", because the Bank of Japan bought them, at peak more than half. The yen paid the price.
