**The argument.** Eating the frog takes energy and attention, and both run out. Look after your body, talk to yourself like an optimist, make your devices work for you instead of the other way round, and give your most important work big, unbroken blocks of time.

## Maximize your personal powers
- **Energy is your capital.** Tiredness is a big cause of procrastination: when you're worn out, you drift to the easy tasks.
- **Your best hours.** Find when you have the most energy, and put your frog there.
- **Rest.** Get enough sleep, take at least one full day a week off work, and take real holidays. Eat well and exercise. If you're exhausted, stop and rest rather than push on.

## Motivate yourself into action
- **Be your own cheerleader.** Most of how you feel comes from how you talk to yourself, so talk to yourself positively.
- **Think like an optimist.** Look for the good in every situation, the lesson in every setback, and the solution to every problem. Think and talk about your goals, not your worries.

## Technology: master or servant
- **A terrible master.** Constant notifications and checking pull you away from your frog, and the urge to check is a habit you can break.
- **A wonderful servant.** Use it to plan, to protect your time, and to block distractions.
- **Be unreachable on purpose.** Turn things off for set periods, don't start the day in your inbox, and check messages at set times.

## Attention and large chunks of time
- **One thing at a time.** Multitasking is really switching between tasks, and every switch costs time and focus.
- **Big blocks.** Important work needs long, unbroken stretches: 60 to 90 minutes, booked like appointments with yourself. Start early, and use travel and waiting time.
- **Putting it together.** Pick one idea from the book, practise it every day until it's automatic, and start every day with your frog.

**The idea worth keeping.** The frog gets eaten by someone rested, positive and undistracted, in a block of time set aside for it.

**Try it on your work.** Book one 90-minute block in tomorrow's plan for your AI service's frog, at your best hour, with your phone in another room and notifications off. Afterwards, note how far you got.
