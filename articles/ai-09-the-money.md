**The idea.** In the base case a €750-a-month service reaches about €6,500 a month after one year and €12,000 after two. A €29-a-month SaaS needs 8 new paying customers every month to reach €3,000 a month by month 24. And every extra €1,000 a month you invest takes years off your FIRE date.

## The service
My assumptions, to be replaced by your real numbers:
- €750 per client per month, €40 of AI tools per client, 3% of clients leaving each month.
- 8 hours to set up each new client. Delivery takes 3 hours per client a month for the first three months, then 1.5 hours once automated.
- **Slow:** a new client every 2 months. **Base:** one a month from month 3. **Strong:** one a month from month 2, two a month from month 7.

Revenue a month:

| Case | Month 6 | Month 12 | Month 24 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slow | €2,120 | €3,885 | €6,581 |
| Base | €2,868 | €6,564 | €12,209 |
| Strong | €3,532 | €11,293 | €23,144 |

Clients, and the hours a month they ask of you:

| Case | Month 6 | Month 12 | Month 24 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slow | 3 · 12 h | 5 · 16 h | 9 · 21 h |
| Base | 4 · 14 h | 9 · 21 h | 16 · 32 h |
| Strong | 5 · 15 h | 15 · 39 h | 31 · 62 h |

Revenue keeps climbing in every case because clients stay. The limit is your hours, not demand: from about month 9 the base case needs more than the 14–16 hours a month that two daily slots give you (part 10).

## The SaaS, for comparison
Using Hormozi's Skool retention: over 20% gone in month 1, about 2% a month after month 6.
- At €29 a month and about 70% gross margin, a paying customer is worth about €277 in gross profit over 24 months.
- At Hormozi's 3:1 rule for a fully automated business, you can spend at most about €92 to win each customer.
- To reach €1,000, €3,000 or €5,000 a month by month 24, you need about 3, 8 or 13 new paying customers every single month.
- Software for one industry can charge €99–299 a month, so it needs fewer customers, but it needs a sales process. That's what the service builds.
- Small SaaS businesses sell for roughly 2.5–4× annual revenue. At €5,000 a month that's €150,000–240,000: helpful, but about a tenth of your FIRE number. [Beancount, citing Acquire.com](https://beancount.io/nl/blog/2026/07/11/bootstrapped-saas-valuation-multiples-2026-acquire-com-indie-founders-guide)

## What it does to your FIRE date
The same maths as your FIRE plan: 5% real return a year, a €1.5M target, starting from €0.

| Invested a month | Years to €1.5M |
| --- | --- |
| €500 | 53 |
| €1,000 | 40 |
| €1,500 | 33 |
| €2,000 | 29 |
| **€3,000** (€1,000 from a job + €2,000 from the business) | **23** |
| €4,000 | 19 |
| €5,000 | 16 |

The service's gross profit is before tax and living costs, so only part of it gets invested. Even so, €1,000 from a job plus €2,000 from the business gets you to €1.5M in about 23 years instead of 40.
