**The idea.** A monthly AI-powered service for one industry scores best: recurring revenue, high margins, a growing market, and a first euro within weeks. Your SaaS idea scores well on margin and simplicity but badly on time and copying, so it works best as step two.

## The scores
Scores run from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong). The first five columns are Hormozi's "perfect business" tests from part 2; the last two are about your situation.

| Option | Sticky | Margin | Growing market | Simple to run | Hard to copy | Fits 14 min a weekday | First euro | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Productized AI service** | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | weeks | **Start here** |
| AI training for teams | 2 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 | weeks | Add-on and lead source |
| Freelance AI building | 1 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 1 | weeks | Fallback only |
| Vertical micro-SaaS | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 2 | months | Year 2, grown from the service |
| Consumer AI apps | 2 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 2 | a lottery | Skip for now |
| Content about your niche | 3 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 6–12 months | Run alongside, small |

What each one is: a **productized service** delivers a fixed result to firms in one industry every month; **training** means half-day workshops for SME teams; **freelance building** means one AI agent per project, at €3,000–15,000; **vertical micro-SaaS** is software for one industry; **content** means building an audience around your niche.

## Why the scores
- **Service, sticky 4:** a monthly deliverable keeps billing; projects end. **Simple 3:** you are in the delivery until it is automated.
- **Service, hard to copy 3:** anyone can buy the same AI. What competitors can't copy is your notes on every client's edge cases, your relationships and your results.
- **Training, sticky 2:** one-off days, so it's a door-opener into the monthly service. **Fits your time 2:** you deliver it live, in office hours.
- **Freelance building, sticky 1:** project work is what Cody Schneider calls "a tar pit". Agent builds in the Netherlands run €3,000–15,000 each ([Informatec Digital](https://informatecdigital.com/nl/guia-completa-sobre-el-coste-y-la-implementacion-de-la-ia-agentica-para-empresas/)), and freelance AI consultants average €120 an hour ([Knab](https://bieb.knab.nl/wat-verdient-een-ai-specialist-zzp-bekijk-uurtarief-en-winst)). Good money, but every month starts at zero.
- **Micro-SaaS, margin 3:** AI products run at roughly 50% gross margin, against 75–85% for classic SaaS, because every request costs inference. [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/have-ai-gross-margins-really-turned-the-corner/)
- **Micro-SaaS, hard to copy 2:** AI coding lets anyone clone a tool in days, and investors marked down per-seat software in the 2026 "SaaSpocalypse". [Betterment](https://www.betterment.com/advisors/resources/ai-software-stocks-market-news-may-2026)
- **Consumer apps:** in the Starter Story data (part 3) they grow through influencers and paid ads, which favours studios with budgets.
- **Content:** builds the proof and brand Hormozi calls the moat, but pays late. Fifteen minutes a day documenting your client work is enough.

## Two paths for later
- **Buy a firm and run it with AI** (an "AI roll-up"). Over 500,000 small Dutch firms are for sale or soon will be. Isenberg's way in is to serve one industry first, then buy from an owner who already trusts you. It needs capital and experience, so years 3–5. [AI Roll Ups](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZT4mpjx0JnE)
- **AI skills in a job.** Companies now hire "forward-deployed engineers" and AI implementers: people who find the work worth automating, test it and roll it out inside the systems the company already uses. The same skill feeds your Career branch, with the 56% wage premium from part 4. [FDE explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zXysLUTLjw4)

**Try it this week.** Score your own SaaS idea on the same seven columns. If "hard to copy" or "fits your time" comes out at 1 or 2, write down which service would teach you what to build.
