**The idea.** Price each offer at about half the value it creates, name one measurable result, and back it with a guarantee. These are starting drafts: your interviews set the real task and price.

## Installers: "No lead left waiting"
- **Every month they get:** every web and phone enquiry answered within 15 minutes; quote follow-ups on day 2 and day 7; a review request after every job; a monthly report.
- **Your AI behind it:** website and phone intake, follow-up messages, review requests.
- **Value to them:** one extra job a month at about €900 profit covers the fee.
- **Price:** €750 a month; the first 3 clients free for 60 days.
- **Guarantee:** if no extra job comes in within the first 60 days, the next month is free.

## Export SME: "Your shop in German in 30 days"
- **Every month they get:** up to 100 product listings and all customer emails in German and French, checked by a native-speaking freelancer; a monthly report on orders from those markets.
- **Your AI behind it:** translation and localisation, tone rules, a glossary per client.
- **Value to them:** cheaper than a translation agency and a German-speaking employee.
- **Price:** €900 a month; the first 3 clients at half price.
- **Guarantee:** listings live within 30 days, or no fee until they are.

## Accountancy firm: "No more chasing documents"
- **Every month they get:** reminders for missing documents drafted and sent, completeness checked, status logged for each client. A person approves before anything goes out.
- **Your AI behind it:** document intake, a reminder agent, a checklist per client.
- **Value to them:** 15–20 hours of assistant time a month, €700–950 at €47.90 an hour.
- **Price:** €600 a month per office.
- **Guarantee:** if they aren't saving 10 hours a month after 60 days, they cancel without paying that month.

## How to set the price and the deal
- **Anchor on the labour it replaces.** Hormozi's point: clients still pay the old price. Luke Sophinos (vertical SaaS) starts at about 50% of the value the product can show and adjusts from feedback. [Vertical SaaS guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IC4MOvYZasU)
- **Name the outcome, not the AI.** "No lead left waiting" sells; "AI automation" doesn't.
- **Offer a premium tier.** Hormozi: have something 10× more expensive available, even if few take it. Here that could be a done-for-you tier that includes running the client's ads.
- **Charge per result where you can measure it.** Hormozi's Allen was paid per appointment that showed up, and Intercom's support AI charges $0.99 per resolved conversation. [Gleap](https://www.gleap.io/blog/intercom-fin-ai-pricing-2026)
- **Do the first 3–5 clients free or cheap,** in exchange for feedback, a testimonial and a referral. That's the "first five customers" note already on your Business shelf, and Hormozi's student playbook. [Best Student Side Hustle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CGSd00h-6zI)

For the name and the guarantee, your *$100M Offers* notes on the Bookshelf go deeper: *Make it stronger: scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees and a good name*.

**Try it this week.** Write your own one-line offer for the niche you lean towards, in this shape: the result as its name, what they get every month, the price, and the guarantee.
