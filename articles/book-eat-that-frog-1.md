**The argument.** There will never be time to do everything. The people who get ahead don't try to; they do the most important task first, before anything else, and make that a habit. Your "frog" is that task: the biggest, most important one, and usually the one you're most likely to put off. And you can't pick it until you're clear what you're trying to achieve.

## The frog
- **The saying.** If the first thing you do each morning is eat a live frog, nothing worse will happen to you all day. Tracy credits it to Mark Twain.
- **Rule one.** If you have to eat two frogs, eat the ugliest one first: start with the hardest, most important task.
- **Rule two.** If you have to eat a live frog, it doesn't pay to sit and look at it for long. Start before you can talk yourself out of it.
- **Decision, discipline, determination.** Decide to do it, make yourself do it, and keep at it until it's a habit.

## Set the table: be clear first
- **Clarity.** The main reason people procrastinate and waste time is that they aren't clear what they want, or in what order. Tracy says only about 3% of adults have clear, written goals, and they get far more done than the rest.
- **Think on paper.** Writing a goal down makes it real, and something you can plan.

## Seven steps to a goal
1. Decide exactly what you want. At work, agree it with whoever you report to.
2. Write it down.
3. Set a deadline, with smaller deadlines on the way.
4. List everything you'll have to do to get there, and add to the list as you think of more.
5. Turn the list into a plan: what comes first, and what depends on what.
6. Act on the plan straight away.
7. Do something every day that moves you toward your main goal.

- **The ten-goals exercise.** Write ten goals for the next year, as if you'd already reached them. Then ask which one would have the biggest positive effect on your life. That's your main goal: plan it, and work on it every day.

**The idea worth keeping.** Get clear on what you want, write it down, and start each day on the one task that matters most, before you can put it off.

**Try it on your work.** Write the one goal for your AI service that would make the biggest difference this year, with a deadline. Then name tomorrow's frog: the task that moves it furthest, and make it the first thing you do.
