**The argument.** Only two kinds of people can tell strangers about your stuff: you, and other people. Lead getters are the other people: customers, employees, agencies and affiliates. They give you leverage, more leads for less of your time. The best of them are customers who refer their friends, and they refer when your product delights them and you ask in the right way.

## Lead getters
- **Leverage.** How much you get for the time you spend. An old lady can lift a truck with a long enough lever; you can let millions know with other people doing it for you.
- **Four scenarios.** You do the Core Four yourself; one lead getter does it for you; many lead getters; then a lead getter who recruits more lead getters. Work goes down as leads go up.
- **The four lead getters.** Customers, employees, agencies and affiliates. You use the Core Four once to get them, and then they use it for you.

## Why referrals grow a business
- **The story.** Hormozi's Facebook ads had been switched off for two weeks before anyone noticed, and the business still made $500,000 a week from word of mouth. On stage, almost all 700 gym owners raised a hand when asked who'd heard about Gym Launch from another owner.
- **Worth more, cost less.** Referred customers buy more, pay more up front and cost almost nothing to get. At 4 to 1 LTGP to CAC, if every customer brings two more, you're at 12 to 1.
- **Exponential.** The Core Four are roughly linear; word of mouth compounds: one brings two, two bring four.
- **The referral growth equation.** The share of customers referred each month minus the share who leave. If referrals beat churn, you grow without advertising; most businesses are on a hamster wheel with the two about equal.
- **Two reasons there are no referrals.** The product isn't as good as you think, and you don't ask.

## Six ways to give more value
- **Goodwill.** The gap between the price and the value people get. Any fool can sell for less; give more value instead.
1. **Sell better customers.** Find what your best customers have in common and target only them. A PR company served only the 15% of clients who were raising money and broke a years-long plateau.
2. **Set better expectations.** Lower your promises bit by bit until your close rate starts to drop, then stop; you'll have room to over-deliver.
3. **Get more people better results.** At Gym Launch, owners who ran ads and made a sale in the first seven days were worth three times as much, so every new customer was pushed to do just that. Make those actions the conditions of your guarantee.
4. **Faster wins.** Seven small deliveries beat one big one. Lasting impressions form in the first 48 hours. Always say when they'll hear from you next ("book a meeting from a meeting"). Add 50% to every timeline and deliver early.
5. **Keep making it better.** Every month: find the most common complaint, fix it with a small group, roll it out, move on to the next.
6. **Tell them what to buy next.** Customers who never referred the first product often refer the bigger one.
- **One question to rule them all.** If every future customer had to come from the one customer you have left, how would you treat them?

## Seven ways to ask for referrals
- **Treat it like an offer.** Dropbox gave free storage to both sides and grew 39 times in 15 months; PayPal gave $10 to each side.
- **One-sided or two-sided.** Pay your cost to acquire a customer to the referrer or the friend, or split it between both. Ask for a three-way introduction, not just a name.
- **Ask right when they buy.** "People who do this with someone else get three times the results. Who else could do this with you?" Ask who, not if. A new salesperson got half their sales this way, and asking at sign-up adds 25 to 30% more sign-ups.
- **A negotiation chip, events, ongoing programmes, unlockable bonuses.** A discount for three introductions; bring-a-friend weeks; talking about doing it together all the time; VIP extras for a referral and a testimonial.
- **The gift card.** Give each customer a gift card worth a third of the programme, for a friend, expiring in 7 to 14 days. Match what you give to what you sell.

**The idea worth keeping.** The best advertising is a happy customer. Build goodwill by delivering more than you promised, then ask for the referral as an offer: who, right when they buy, with something in it for both.

**Try it on your work.** For your AI service, write down how you'd treat the one customer every future customer had to come from. Then write the referral ask you'd use at sign-up, with what's in it for both sides.
